LifeLink

A place to
help you remember

LifeLink is a web app that is designed for people who are facing prosopagnosia, a condition that makes it difficult to recognize faces. The app allows users to create a personalized database of people they know, complete with photos and notes about each person. This can help users remember important details about their friends, family, and acquaintances, making social interactions easier and more enjoyable.



Our Mission?

Our mission is to make it easier for people with prosopagnosia to navigate the world and maintain meaningful relationships.

How it works?

Using LifeLink is simple and intuitive. You can add people to your database, upload their photos, and add notes about them. The app will help you remember important details about each person, making social interactions easier and more enjoyable.

Questions?

If you have any questions about LifeLink, please feel free to contact us!
