# LifeLink

**Helping people with prosopagnosia keep track of the people in their lives.**

# About the Project

LifeLink is a web application designed to help people with prosopagnosia, also known as face blindness. Prosopagnosia can make it difficult to recognize or remember people's faces, which can create challenges when interacting with family members and other people.

LifeLink aims to make these interactions easier by allowing users to organize information about people they know and understand how those people are related to one another.

# Features

- **People Management:** Add and organize information about people in your life.
- **Family Tree:** View family relationships in a visual family tree to understand how different people are connected.
- **Search:** Find saved people more easily.
- **Personal Notes:** Record helpful details about individuals, such as their voice, hairstyle, accessories, and other distinguishing characteristics.
- **Photos:** Associate photos with people to provide additional visual context.
- **User Accounts:** Sign up and log in to access the application's account features.

# Purpose

The goal of LifeLink is to provide a practical organizational tool for people with prosopagnosia. By combining personal notes, photos, and family relationships in one place, the application aims to help users recall useful information about people they know.

LifeLink is an assistive tool, not a medical treatment or a replacement for professional support.
