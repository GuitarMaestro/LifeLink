LifeLink

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LifeLink handles information when you use it. We designed this with privacy in mind, particularly because it may be used to help people remember faces and relationships.

## 1. Information We Collect Depending on how you use the App, we may collect: - **Photos** that you choose to add to the App. - **Names or nicknames** that you enter for people. - **Relationship information**, such as family or friend relationships that you choose to create. - **Notes or descriptions** that you voluntarily enter. - **App settings and preferences** necessary to provide the App's features. We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless you voluntarily enter it into the App.

## 2. Photos and Face Information The App may allow you to add photographs of people to help you recognize and remember them. Photos are provided by you voluntarily. We do not sell photographs or use them for advertising. Unless specifically stated otherwise within the App, photos and related information are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. We do not use photographs to create, train, or improve facial-recognition or artificial-intelligence models.

## 3. How We Use Information Information you provide may be used to: - Display people and their relationships within the App. - Provide memory and recognition exercises. - Save your preferences and progress. - Improve the functionality and reliability of the App. - Respond to support requests if you contact us. We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Information Sharing We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your photos, names, relationship information, or notes with advertisers. If information must be shared with a service provider to operate a specific feature, we will limit the information shared to what is reasonably necessary for that feature. We may disclose information when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of our users, the App, or others.

## 5. Data Storage We aim to minimize the amount of personal information stored outside your device. Where information is stored locally, you can remove it by deleting the relevant information from the App or uninstalling the App, subject to how your device handles application data and backups. If the App later introduces cloud storage or accounts, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what information is stored, why it is stored, and how users can delete it.

## 6. Children's Privacy The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental or guardian involvement. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate permission, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

## 7. Security We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no electronic storage system or method of transmitting information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should avoid entering highly sensitive information into the App unless it is necessary for your use of the App.

## 8. Third-Party Services The App may use third-party services for functions such as analytics, crash reporting, authentication, cloud storage, or other technical services. If third-party services are added, this Privacy Policy will be updated where appropriate to explain what information those services receive.

## 9. Your Choices You can choose what information to add to the App. You may also: - Remove photos you have added. - Remove people or relationship information. - Delete notes you have created. - Stop using the App at any time. - Uninstall the App. If the App provides an account or cloud-storage feature in the future, additional deletion and account-management options may be provided.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the App changes. When we make significant changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date and, where appropriate, provide additional notice within the App.
